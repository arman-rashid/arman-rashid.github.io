Research Focus

Instrumentation & methodology for molecular electronics

Designing novel methods to explore charge transport across metal(carbon) │ molecule (or SAM) │ metal(carbon) junctions under external bias or temperature gradient, and evaluating how transport through single molecules and hybrid assemblies is modulated by external stimuli — light, magnetic field, and chemical gating. At KIT, this work centers on a liquid-helium-temperature scanning tunnelling microscope, developing advanced instrumentation to study light–matter interactions and quantum transport at the nanoscale.
